ABOUT THE ORGANISATION

A regional platform for micronations.

The Association of Southeast Asian Micronations, abbreviated as ASEAM, is an intermicronational regional organisation representing and connecting micronations in Southeast Asia.

The organisation was established following discussions among its founding members on 4 September 2026 and was formally established on 5 September 2026. It was created to provide a more active platform for cooperation and regional dialogue following inactivity from MASA and SEAMA.
