About the organisation

A regional platform for micronations.

The Association of Southeast Asian Micronations (ASEAM) is an intermicronational regional organisation representing and connecting micronations in Southeast Asia.

The organisation was established following discussions among its founding members on 4 September 2026 and was formally established on 5 September 2026. It provides an active platform for cooperation and regional dialogue.

Founding document

The Treaty of Bandar Mewar

The Treaty of Bandar Mewar sets out the ratification, deposit and notification procedures governing the founding treaty of ASEAM.

It enters into force 30 days after the third founding state deposits its Instrument of Ratification. Additional states may then join by depositing an Instrument of Accession, subject to approval by the ASEAM Assembly.
