About ASEAM

A platform for regional micronational cooperation

ASEAM brings together Southeast Asian micronations through dialogue, diplomacy, cultural exchange and shared regional initiatives. It provides members with a formal platform to develop friendly relations and pursue mutual goals.

Headquarters: Bandar Mewar, Federal Republic of Huguma

Objectives

What ASEAM works to achieve

Regional diplomacy

Maintain constructive relations and regular dialogue among members.

Peace and friendship

Resolve disagreements respectfully and strengthen mutual trust.

Cultural exchange

Celebrate the identities, traditions and creativity of member states.

Shared development

Support useful projects that benefit the wider ASEAM community.

International presence

Represent Southeast Asian micronational interests responsibly.

Youth and sport

Create accessible activities such as the SEAM Games to unite citizens.

Founding charter

The Treaty of Bandar Mewar

The Treaty of Bandar Mewar is the founding document of ASEAM. It defines the association's commitment to sovereignty, cooperation, peaceful relations and collective progress.
